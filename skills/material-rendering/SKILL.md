# Material Rendering

Describe materials by observable surface behavior.

## Material cues
- Velvet: directional nap, soft light absorption, subtle pile variation.
- Woven fabric: visible weave, restrained microtexture, natural folds.
- Leather: grain, controlled specular response, realistic creasing.
- Wood: grain direction, pores, finish and edge treatment.
- Metal: reflections, brushed direction, roughness and edge highlights.
- Glass: transparency, refraction and reflections.
- Stone/concrete: granular variation and realistic roughness.

Do not turn fabric into plastic, erase weave, exaggerate texture or invent material properties. Preserve the source material's color unless instructed otherwise.
