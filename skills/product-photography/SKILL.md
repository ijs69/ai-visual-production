# Product Photography

Create commercially useful images that make the product clear, desirable and truthful.

## Principles
- Product identity and proportions take priority over dramatic styling.
- Use lighting to reveal shape and material, not conceal defects.
- Keep the product readable at thumbnail size.
- Use controlled highlights and believable contact shadows.
- Choose background contrast that separates the product without competing with it.
- Leave intentional negative space for layouts or advertising copy when requested.

## Furniture-specific checks
Inspect silhouette, seat depth, backrest height, armrests, cushion arrangement, legs, upholstery seams, piping, buttons, mechanisms and visible frame details.

## Shot types
Front/three-quarter hero, side profile, rear view, detail close-up, mechanism close-up, material close-up and lifestyle context. Each angle must depict the same product consistently.
