# Camera & Composition

Specify viewpoint before choosing lens language.

## Consider
- Front, side, rear, top-down, low-angle, eye-level or three-quarter view.
- Camera height and tilt.
- Subject-to-camera distance.
- Perspective compression or exaggeration.
- Crop, margins and negative space.
- Horizontal, vertical or square framing.
- Focal-length character only when it helps communicate perspective.

Avoid using wide-angle perspective for products that must retain accurate proportions. Do not request a new angle if the source image does not support a reliable reconstruction; disclose uncertainty.
