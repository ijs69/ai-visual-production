# Image Quality Control

Review the generated/edited result against the user's brief and source references.

## Checklist
- Correct subject and product identity.
- Geometry and proportions preserved.
- No missing, duplicated or invented parts.
- Camera angle and crop match the brief.
- Materials and textures look plausible.
- Lighting and shadows are coherent.
- Background is clean and intentional.
- No visible artifacts, halos, warping or melted details.
- Color is accurate.
- Composition supports the intended commercial use.
- Output format and aspect ratio are correct.

## Report
Classify findings as:
- **Pass** — meets the requirement.
- **Needs correction** — visible defect that can likely be fixed.
- **Uncertain** — cannot be verified from available image/reference.

Do not claim pixel-perfect accuracy or a specific resolution unless verified.
