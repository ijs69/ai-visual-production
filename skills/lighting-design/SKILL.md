# Lighting Design

Describe light in terms of source, direction, size, distance, softness and intensity.

## Common setup
- Key light: establishes form and primary direction.
- Fill: controls contrast and shadow density.
- Rim/back light: separates the subject from the background.
- Bounce/reflector: lifts selected shadow areas.
- Background light: controls background tone independently.

## Review
Check highlight clipping, muddy shadows, inconsistent shadow directions, floating objects, excessive edge glow and unnatural reflections.

Do not add multiple light sources that imply contradictory shadow directions. Keep lighting appropriate to the material.
