# Visual Production References

## Reusable prompt template
**Task:** [generate / edit / retouch / replace background / create detail crop]  
**Subject:** [exact product and identifying features]  
**Preserve:** [geometry, dimensions, construction, materials, colors, stitching, hardware]  
**Camera:** [viewpoint, height, distance, perspective, framing]  
**Composition:** [placement, crop, negative space, orientation]  
**Lighting:** [key source, direction, size, softness, fill, shadow behavior]  
**Environment:** [background and contextual elements]  
**Materials:** [surface response, texture, reflections, folds]  
**Finish:** [commercial realism, color treatment, detail level]  
**Output:** [aspect ratio, intended use, verified format]  
**Avoid:** [only relevant likely failure modes]

## Quality checklist
- Product identity is unchanged.
- Geometry and proportions are preserved.
- No components are missing, duplicated or invented.
- Seams, stitching, buttons, legs and mechanisms are correct.
- Material and color remain faithful.
- Camera angle and crop match the brief.
- Lighting and shadows are coherent.
- Background is clean and intentional.
- No halos, warping, melted details or repeated textures.
- Composition works at the intended display size.
- Output format and aspect ratio are correct.
