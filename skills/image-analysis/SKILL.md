# Image Analysis

Analyze the visible image before proposing edits.

## Inspect
- Main subject and secondary objects.
- Silhouette, geometry, proportions and orientation.
- Camera viewpoint, perspective and crop.
- Composition, subject placement and negative space.
- Light direction, softness, intensity, highlights and shadows.
- Background, color palette and tonal range.
- Materials, surface texture, seams, edges and small details.
- Focus, depth of field, noise, compression and visible defects.

## Report format
1. **What is visible** — objective observations.
2. **What works** — useful existing qualities.
3. **Potential issues** — only defects supported by the image.
4. **Preserve** — details that must not change.
5. **Possible edits** — changes aligned with the user's request.

Do not infer hidden construction or exact material composition from appearance alone.
