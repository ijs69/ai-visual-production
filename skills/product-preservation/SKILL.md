# Product Preservation

This skill overrides stylistic preferences when editing an existing product image.

## Protected attributes by default
- Overall silhouette and geometry.
- Width, height, depth and relative proportions.
- Number, shape and placement of components.
- Seams, stitching, piping, buttons and upholstery panels.
- Legs, feet, handles, hinges and mechanisms.
- Material identity, texture and original color.
- Brand marks, labels and distinctive details.

## Allowed changes
Only changes explicitly requested by the user, such as background, lighting, crop, environment or specified camera treatment.

## Verification
Compare source and output feature by feature. Flag any altered, missing or invented component. If the output changes product identity, do not present it as a faithful product edit.
